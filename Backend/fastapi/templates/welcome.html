What is CineFlow

One Telegram bot, one Stremio addon

The library lives on Telegram and CineFlow exposes it to Stremio (or Nuvio) as a private, token-protected addon — so streaming feels like any other catalog, with none of the manual file hunting.

Movies & TV, organized

Every title is catalogued with posters, quality tags, and episode breakdowns — browsable like a real streaming catalog.

One private link

Your subscription gives you a personal addon link — install it once in Stremio and every new upload shows up automatically.

Subtitles included

Matching subtitles are attached automatically, so playback starts ready to watch without extra setup.

Quality control

Pick the resolution that fits your connection — from data-friendly to full quality — right from the player.

Request what's missing

Can't find a title? Send a request straight from the bot and it gets added to the queue for review.

Private by design

Every addon link is tied to your subscription token — nothing is public, and access can be revoked any time.

Installation

Add the addon to Stremio or Nuvio

Full walkthrough for both apps — pick whichever you use.

Stremio

  1. Install Stremio on your phone, TV, or computer from stremio.com (or your device's app store) and create a free Stremio account.
  2. Open the CineFlow bot on Telegram and subscribe to a plan.
  3. The bot replies with your private, personal addon link — copy it.
  4. In Stremio, open the puzzle-piece Addons icon in the top bar.
  5. Paste your link into the search field at the top of the Addons page and press enter.
  6. Click Install on the CineFlow addon card that appears.
  7. Your library now shows up under Discover/Board, and search results include your titles.
Download Stremio

Nuvio

  1. Install Nuvio on your device from nuvio.tv (or the linked APK/store page for your platform).
  2. Open the CineFlow bot on Telegram and subscribe to a plan.
  3. The bot replies with your private, personal addon link — copy it.
  4. In Nuvio, open Settings → Addons and select Add Addon.
  5. Paste your link into the Addon URL field, then select Install Addon.
  6. Your CineFlow library now appears when you browse or search inside Nuvio.

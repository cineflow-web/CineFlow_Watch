Setup guide

Getting CineFlow into Stremio or Nuvio

A closer look at installing your addon link — step by step, for either app.

Stremio

  1. Install Stremio from stremio.com (desktop, Android, or Android TV) and create a free account if you don't have one.
  2. Open the CineFlow bot on Telegram and subscribe to a plan.
  3. The bot replies with your private, personal addon link — copy it in full.
  4. In Stremio, open the puzzle-piece Addons icon in the top bar.
  5. Paste your link into the search field at the top of the Addons page and press enter.
  6. Click Install on the CineFlow addon card that appears.
  7. Your library shows up under Discover/Board, and search results include your titles.
Download Stremio

Nuvio

  1. Install Nuvio on your device from nuvio.to (or the linked APK/store page).
  2. Open the CineFlow bot on Telegram and subscribe to a plan.
  3. The bot replies with your private, personal addon link — copy it in full.
  4. In Nuvio, go to Settings → Addons → Add Addon.
  5. Paste your link into the field and confirm to save it.
  6. Your CineFlow library appears the next time you browse or search inside Nuvio.

Common questions

My addon link stopped working — why?

Links are tied to your active subscription. If a plan lapses, the link is disabled until it's renewed — message the bot to check your status or renew.

Can I use the same link on more than one device?

Yes — install it in Stremio or Nuvio on as many of your own devices as you like. It's the same catalog everywhere.

Nothing shows up after installing — what now?

Double check the full link was pasted with nothing cut off, then restart the app. If it still doesn't appear, reach out through the Telegram bot.

Do subtitles need to be installed separately?

No — matching subtitles are attached automatically wherever available, so playback is ready without extra addons.
